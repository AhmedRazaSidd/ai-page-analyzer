Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

PagePilot AI — Chrome Extension

1. Overview

PagePilot AI ("the Extension") is an intelligent Chrome Extension that lets you interact with web pages using AI. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

2. Data We Collect

  • Page Content: When you explicitly click "Analyze Page" or use a Quick Action, the visible text content of the active tab is extracted and sent to our backend server for AI processing. This is done on-demand only — the Extension does not automatically read pages in the background.
  • Your Messages: The questions and prompts you type in the chat interface are stored in our PostgreSQL database linked to your anonymous session.
  • Anonymous Session ID: A session identifier (cookie) is stored locally and in our database to associate your conversation history with your browser session. No name, email, or account is required to use the Extension.
  • Page URL and Title: The URL and title of the analyzed page are stored as metadata for conversation context.

3. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your personal identity, name, or email (for anonymous users).
  • We do not track your browsing activity or history outside of explicit analysis requests.
  • We do not sell, share, or monetize your data to any third party.
  • We do not use your data to train AI models.

4. How We Use Your Data

Collected data is used exclusively to:

  • Generate AI responses via the Groq API (page content is sent to Groq for inference).
  • Store and retrieve your conversation history within the Extension.
  • Maintain your session across browser restarts.

5. Third-Party Services

Page content and your messages are processed by Groq for AI inference. Groq's privacy policy governs their handling of this data. We do not share your data with any other third parties.

6. Data Storage & Retention

Conversation data is stored in a PostgreSQL database on our backend server. Anonymous session data is retained as long as your session cookie is valid. You may delete your conversation history at any time from within the Extension.

7. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You can:

  • Delete individual conversations from the sidebar inside the Extension.
  • Clear all your data by clearing browser cookies (which invalidates your session).
  • Request full data deletion by contacting us at privacy@ai-page-assistant.example.com.

8. Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS. Anonymous session tokens are used to authenticate requests. We do not store payment information or sensitive personal data.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the Extension constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact us at: privacy@ai-page-assistant.example.com
